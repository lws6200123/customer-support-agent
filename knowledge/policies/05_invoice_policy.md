## Scope and provenance

This document paraphrases public JD.com invoice guidance for a DemoShop portfolio workflow. It is not tax advice and does not claim that DemoShop or Olist uses JD.com invoice systems.

## Invoice request

Collect the order, invoice type, title type, title text, requested content, and any legally required organization information. Invoice amount must be grounded in the recorded eligible paid amount; do not infer tax treatment from an item category alone.

Electronic invoices may be made available after the relevant order/payment completion event. Order completion state matters because available request and modification paths can differ before and after completion.

## Title and content

The public reference distinguishes personal and organization titles and requires complete, accurate information. Specialized invoice types can require additional validated organization and banking details. Customer service should not invent missing tax identifiers, addresses, or account information.

## Modification handling

Before completion, supported fields may be editable through the order path. After completion, supported changes may use a dedicated invoice path. The set of changeable fields depends on invoice type and current state; unsupported changes, seller-issued invoices, incomplete records, or special tax handling require customer service or the responsible seller.

Returns may require cancellation/red-letter treatment and reissue according to applicable invoicing rules. Explain the process without claiming that a refund and an invoice adjustment are the same operation.
