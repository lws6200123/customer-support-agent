## Scope and provenance

This is a paraphrased reference for the DemoShop portfolio simulation. The source material is public JD.com help content, while transaction facts come separately from anonymized Olist data. No shared real-company origin is implied.

## Intake categories

Classify the reported problem before proposing a path:

- functional defect or quality issue;
- visible or concealed delivery damage;
- missing product, accessory, or package component;
- wrong model, specification, quantity, or product-description mismatch;
- warranty, repair, return, or exchange question.

## Evidence and inspection

Collect the order and item identity, symptom, first-observed time, package condition, affected components, and relevant photos or video. For technical products, an authorized inspection or repair record may be necessary. When no local inspection channel exists, route the case for assisted inspection rather than inventing a result.

For delivery damage or missing components, the public reference expects prompt reporting; the single canonical reference threshold is `business_rules.yaml.public_reference_parameters.damaged_or_missing_report_hours`.

## Product-specific cautions

Mobile, digital, and IT products may have category-specific limits. Activation codes, software licenses, anti-counterfeit codes, device activation, customer-stored data, unauthorized repair, or damage from misuse can change eligibility. Customers should be advised to back up personal data before repair because support handling should not promise preservation of device data.

The product page, warranty terms, applicable consumer protections, manufacturer rules, and inspection findings may each affect the available remedy. General return language must not override a specific category rule.

## Resolution pathways

Depending on verified facts, the next step may be troubleshooting, manufacturer service, inspection, repair, return, exchange, replacement of missing components, or human review. Customer service must explain what evidence is missing and avoid promising a remedy before eligibility is established.

Any money movement or sensitive account action remains subject to the machine-readable DemoShop internal controls in `business_rules.yaml`.
